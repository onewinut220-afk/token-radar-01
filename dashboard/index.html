How the ranking works

1. Start from every protocol in the snapshot (9,100). 2. Keep only those with no token: no symbol, no contract address, no CoinGecko id and no market cap. 3. Drop TVL under $1M, and drop categories whose product is itself a tradable token (liquid staking, bridges, wrapped assets), centralised exchanges, front-ends and chains. 4. Merge version rows into one family (for example, a V2 and a V3 entry become one row). 5. Implied FDV = TVL × the median market-cap / TVL of tokenised peers in the same category (global median where a category has fewer than three peers), capped at $5B. Curator and allocator TVL is haircut 25% because it double-counts capital already counted in the underlying protocols. 6. Likelihood (5%–95%) scores size, 7-day TVL growth, audits, multi-chain presence, age and the category's token-launch precedent. 7. Implied value = implied FDV × likelihood. The board is sorted by implied value.

What this radar cannot see

DefiLlama TVL misses products with no on-chain TVL: wallets, social apps and marketplaces (their launches may be large, but there is no TVL to anchor a number to). Market cap here is circulating market cap, a conservative stand-in for fully diluted value. A high implied value is not a promise of an airdrop — no protocol on this board has announced a token. Farm with small amounts, use a fresh wallet, and never sign a transaction from a link in a DM or an ad.
